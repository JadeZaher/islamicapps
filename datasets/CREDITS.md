## Strategies & methodologies (1)

### Uniform Taḥwīl-Aware Isnad Extraction (Tier 1)

- **Status:** active
- **Applies to:** sunni, shia-imami, zaydi, ibadi
- **Consumes sources:** openiti, sunnah-com, thaqalayn-api, sheikahmad-pure-canon
- **Introduced:** 2026-06-09 · **Last reviewed:** 2026-06-16
- **Implementation:** datasets/hadith-data/extract_isnad_unified.py, datasets/hadith-data/regen_unified_csvs.py, datasets/hadith-data/algo_candidates/, datasets/hadith-data/algo_evaluate.py, datasets/hadith-data/audit_tahweel_stats.py, conductor/tracks/uniform_isnad_extraction_20260609/spec.md

**Problem.** Classical hadith isnads frequently converge: a single matn (main report
text) can be transmitted via multiple parallel chains that merge at a
shared narrator partway up the chain. The convergence point is marked
in the manuscript with the letter ح (taḥwīl, "switch"). A naive parser
that ignores ح collapses what are in reality N independent chains into
one corrupted sequence, which then poisons downstream mutawātir
detection, narrator-reliability inference, and chain-grading.

**Evaluation.** Hand-annotated gold-set of 120 hadiths (30 per tradition, balanced
across 0/1/2/3+ taḥwīl-branch counts). Three candidate algorithms
baked off (A: greedy split, B: regex-anchor, C: LCS-suffix). Metrics:
branch-count accuracy (exact match to gold), per-branch Jaccard
overlap on narrator sequences (median + p10/p90). → Candidate C (LCS-suffix) selected:
  branch-count accuracy: 1.000
  Jaccard median:        0.845
  Jaccard p90:           0.730
  Jaccard p10:           0.972
Candidate A: 0.71 branch-count accuracy (lost branches on nested ح).
Candidate B: 0.94 branch-count accuracy but Jaccard median 0.61
  (false-positive splits on non-taḥwīl ح).

**Details:** [`datasets/strategies/uniform-tahweel-aware-isnad-extraction.md`](./strategies/uniform-tahweel-aware-isnad-extraction.md)
