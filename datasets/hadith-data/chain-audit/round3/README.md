# Chain audit, round 3: independent re-check after "chain builder round 3" (commits `cc91b0e`, `c381cc1`, `f6a73fe`; projection `2026.11.22.8`)

Read-only against production; only this directory was written; nothing deleted; no process left running. Method, definitions and tooling are those of `../round2/README.md` (strict = missing, false, merged or split link, non-narrator stored as a name, wrongly inherited chain; any = strict plus names over-extended by a verb, gloss, honorific or date, truncated letters; Imam-not-stored after «قال سألت أبا عبد الله» is a convention gap, not a defect). Every sampled item was read by me against `text_ar`/`sanad_ar` (parent text for inheriting reports); none was counted clean unread. Pure Canon (Ahmad, Darimi, Muwatta) is judged against the source's own `sanad` field, as in round 2.

## Result in seven lines

1. **Sunni did not improve and is not better than round 2**: strict 18/100 = 18.0% (11.7-26.7) against 17.3% (round 2) and 10.7% (earlier lane). K6 text works alone 17/67 = 25.4% (round 2: 20.9%). The mix changed again: truncated or headless routes fell (round 2 K6 about 10/67, now 3/67 plus 1 report-level) but **false links, duplicated links and matn promoted to narrators rose**.
2. **The seam "restored whole" population is the worst thing measured**: 10/14 = 71% defective (flattened routes with «ح»), and the surface-cut population 14/21 = 67%.
3. **Imami got worse on the same definition once everything is read**: 17/100 = 17.0% (10.9-25.5) against 9.1% (round 2, 31 chains unread = clean) and 15.5% (earlier lane). Where the fix reached it works: al-Kafi 3/50 = 6.0%, and the new «A عن B و C عن D عن E» rule matched Noor in 3 of 3 checked cases. The damage is in the small works (Khisal, Duafa, Ghayba, Saduq, Mujam: 11/32 = 34%) and Persian-ya texts.
4. **Rabi 22.5%, Zayd 2.5%, Rida 0%** at report level (round 2 was chain level: 2.5% / 0% / 12.5%, not comparable). Rida inheritance is fixed (0/40, and 0/11 of the changed set). Rabi still has reports with a written isnad and no chain (4/40) and regained chains that are wrong (6/31 read).
5. **Of the round-2 regression rows, 292 of 373 now read correct, 34 were never regressions, 47 are open**; but in a random 60 of the "fixed" ones I disagree with 8 (13.3%, 6.9-24.2).
6. Two fixes the fixer announced as done are only partly done: «غير واحد» (still 75 split halves; the rule does not match Persian ya) and chapter headings / rijal entries stored as chains.
7. Only 4 reports were compared with an online edition (Noor 4, Turath 2); the rest is reading against the Arabic.
