# What we hold that bears on a report's chain, text and metadata

Read from the files and the production database (read-only, 2026-11-22), not from memory. Short codes are the archive's own
(`src/lib/sources/registry.ts`, the /sources page): **THQ** Thaqalayn (CC BY 4.0), **OITI** OpenITI (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0), **SUN**
sunnah.com (owner permission), **NMC** NabiMuhammad.com Pure Canon (owner permission), **MSD** Muslim Scholars Database / the narrator
lexicon (owner permission), **K6I** the K6 dataset's narrator index (owner permission), **RIJ** OpenITI rijal texts (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0),
**HKG** hadith-kg (CC BY 4.0), **RWY** Riwayat corpus (CC0 layer), **SWI** this archive's own layer. A report is keyed
`hadith:<record_id>`; record ids are `k6:<n>`, `thaqalayn:<hash>`, `pure-canon:pc_<n>`, `openiti:<tradition>:<id>|<no>|<page>`.

## What v2 packets use, and what they leave out (and why)

- **Used as claims about the chain**: K6I (#2), HKG (#4) and RWY (#5) read **raw from their own databases** (not the claims aligned to our
  mentions, #10/#11, which our stored chain shaped), THQ's English sanad (#6), NMC's chain-field statement (#8), neighbours (#13), correspondences (#12).
- **Used as facts about names**: MSD lexicon rows reached **through the K6 id** (#3), HKG `rawis` and Riwayat `narrator` fields as the dataset gives
  them. The Sunni lexicon is never offered for an Imami chain: no Imami source links an Imami name to a lexicon row, so Imami name facts
  come only from Riwayat's own narrator records. For Ibadi and Zaydi reports the lexicon is not used (hadith-kg's own record is);
  `external_narrator_lexicon_links` (#10) is the route to add a lexicon fact there later, only where a source links it.
- **Left out**: the rijal layer (#9): every link is `candidate_only`, most are ambiguous (`candidate_count` > 1, `selected` false), and
  Imami name shapes are short and shared, so a verdict or date shown for the wrong namesake would mislead more than it helps. Trigger to add: reviewed links
  (`narrator_same_as`) for the pilot's Imami narrators. Also left out: hadith-kg's `rank` and `hukum` wording (the registry forbids showing them as verdicts), the stored chain and our
  candidates (#11) for the first two readers.
