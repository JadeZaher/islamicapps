# v3.1 holds: why a reading is queued, not applied

`holds_v3_1.compute_holds(reading, output, text, context, stored_surfaces, other_output)` returns `[{"code", "detail"}]` (`stored_surfaces` is kept for the callers and no longer used). A hold never changes or discards a reading. Agreement is
`expand_v3_1.canonical()` equality. Hits below are on the agreed readings of batch 001 (the v3 outputs, re-triaged: `batch-001/RETRIAGE.md`); right / wrong are the batch-001 judge's, adjusted to the owner's rulings of 2026-11-22. **The new detectors were written
after reading those cases: the hit counts are in-sample, the first tranche's judge sample measures them.** Codes of v3 not listed here (`cannot_read_without_previous_report`, `needs_ruling` when both readers set it, `too_many_routes`, `nested_jami`, `nested_semicolon`, `back_reference_only`, `second_isnad_not_same_saying`) are unchanged
except where the table says.

`holds_v3_1.empty_reading_state(text)` returns `("transmission_name", why)`, `("unsettled_opening", why)` or `("applicable", "")`; `triage_v3_1` turns an eligible empty reading into `applies: no_chain`. The opening classes (`opening_class`): `pronoun`, `isnad_phrase`, `bare_an`, `asker_pronoun`,
`asker_named`, `implicit_speaker`, `first_person`, `collective`; `backref_lib_v3_1.cjs` has the same regexes (`tests/opening_cases_v3_1.json` runs both).

Not built: a hold on the four judged-undecided eligible readings (a judgment of the Imam, a narrated encounter, two sayings, a cited book's author: no deterministic cue; they are the owner's open rulings and the reader's `needs_ruling` is their only guard); the 95cd8f class (a restated name that is longer than the first: «سبيعة بنت الحارث الأسلمية» / «سبيعة») is not folded.
