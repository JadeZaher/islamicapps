# Product Guide

## Vision

Democratize access to Islamic scholarly resources by building a research-grade, open-source hadith knowledge platform that makes the depth of classical Islamic scholarship — isnad analysis, narrator criticism, historical context, and comparative religious studies — accessible to serious Muslims worldwide without requiring institutional access or specialized training.

## Target Users

**Primary: Serious Muslims seeking scholarly depth**
- Practicing Muslims who want to engage with hadith authenticity on a rigorous level
- Self-educated students of Islamic sciences
- Educators and da'wah workers who need credible sourced material
- Researchers (academic or independent) studying hadith, isnad, and cross-cultural religious transmission

**Secondary: Open-source contributors and builders**
- Developers who want to extend or fork the platform
- Data contributors with Islamic studies expertise (gated, invite-only write access)

## Core Problems Solved

1. **Access barrier**: Classical hadith scholarship is locked behind Arabic literacy, expensive books, or institutional library access. This app surfaces that scholarship in a navigable, visual format.
2. **Isnad opacity**: Most hadith databases show text only. This app visualizes the transmission chain graph, narrator reliability, and chain health — making the *why* behind grading visible.
3. **Historical vacuum**: Hadiths are often decontextualized. The app links narrators to historical events, geographic regions, and timelines.
4. **Comparative ignorance**: The scholarly field of Isra'iliyyat and cross-cultural hadith studies is almost entirely absent from public-facing Islamic tools. This app makes those connections visible and debatable.

## Key Features

- **Isnad graph visualization** — interactive transmission chain graphs with narrator reliability overlays
- **Dual grading system** — auto-calculated grade from chain analysis + scholar verdict override
- **Narrator profiles** — biography, tabaqah, geographic region, teacher-student network, historical timeline
- **Comparative religious studies** — cross-cultural parallels with Jewish, Christian, and Zoroastrian sources (Isra'iliyyat classification: Muwafiq / Mukhalif / Maskut Anhu)
- **Historical context** — events, locations, and narrator timelines
- **Scholarly commentaries** — sourced commentary linked to specific hadiths or narrators
- **Open source** — full codebase available for reuse, forking, and extension

## Access Model

| Layer | Access |
|---|---|
| Public read | All visualizations, hadith text, isnad graphs, comparative studies |
| Gated contributions | Invite-only write access for data contributors |
| Open source | Full codebase MIT/open licensed for anyone to deploy |

## Success Metrics

- Hadiths fully enriched with isnad graphs, narrator data, and historical context
- Cross-cultural parallels catalogued for all major Isra'iliyyat-studied hadith categories
- Contribution pipeline operational (automated suggestions + human review)
- Public usability: non-Arabic-speakers can navigate and understand scholarly grading
