About Us
Thaqalayn Foundation works to preserve, digitize, and widen access to classical Islamic textual heritage, with a particular focus on Quran and hadith literature.

1. Foundation2. Mission3. Library4. Editorial Approach5. Contact1. Foundation
We are Thaqalayn Foundation (''Stichting Thaqalayn''), a registered foundation in the Netherlands, dedicated to the preservation and dissemination of Islamic textual heritage.

2. Mission and Objectives
At Thaqalayn, our core mission is the preservation, digitization, and worldwide dissemination of Islamic textual heritage, with a special focus on foundational texts such as the Quran and Hadith literature. Thaqalayn strives to promote intercultural dialogue and academic research by making these vital sources more accessible to a global audience.
Platform
Developing and maintaining the Thaqalayn Hadith Library, our digital platform and mobile application.

Indexing and Search
Indexing, and where appropriate, translating and making searchable a vast array of Islamic heritage materials, including significant Hadith collections.

Translation
Offering translations of classical texts into multiple languages, with an initial focus on English, to broaden understanding and accessibility.

Access to Knowledge
Championing the broader cause of promoting access to knowledge for all.

3. The Thaqalayn Hadith Library
The Thaqalayn Hadith Library, a key initiative of Thaqalayn, is your source for exploring the classical hadith tradition of the Imāmī Shia Muslim school of thought. We believe that access to primary historical sources is a fundamental right, and our goal is to make it easier for everyone to access and study these important sources of faith and wisdom.
Our platform indexes a wide range of classical Imāmī hadith texts, including well-known collections and lesser-known works, from various scholars and transmitters. We use already available translations to make these texts available in Arabic and English side by side. We hope that this, in combination with the versatile search engine, can turn this platform into an aid for English-speaking researchers, students, and scholars.

4. Editorial Approach and Scope
Educational Purpose
The texts on this website are presented for educational and academic purposes only. The views and interpretations contained in these texts may not reflect the views of Thaqalayn, the translators, or the website administration. As with any religious or historical text, one should always refer to experts in the field to understand the authenticity or implication of a specific text or book.

Selection and Curation
The books included on our website are a curated selection assembled under the advice of scholars. Thaqalayn strives to present the most authentic and reliable texts and has consulted with experts in the field to make a humble attempt at this.
