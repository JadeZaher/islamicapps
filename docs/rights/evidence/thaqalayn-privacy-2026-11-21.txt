Privacy Policy
This page explains how Thaqalayn.com collects, uses, discloses, retains, and protects personal data for the website, user accounts, and ChatGPT plugin.

Effective Date: April 28, 2026
Last Updated: November 5, 2026

1. Introduction2. What We Collect3. Retention4. Sharing5. International Transfers6. Security7. Cookies8. Your Rights9. Changes10. Contact1. Introduction
Welcome to Thaqalayn.com ("we", "us", "our"). We are committed to protecting the privacy of our users and visitors ("you", "your"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, retain, and protect personal data in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).
The data controller responsible for your personal data is Thaqalayn.com, contactable via info@thaqalayn.com.

2. What Personal Data We Collect and Why
We collect and process the following categories of personal data for the following purposes and based on the following legal bases:
a. Account Registration
Data: username, email address, password (stored in a hashed format).
Purpose: to create and manage your account, enable you to log in, and provide you with the associated services of our website.
Legal Basis: performance of a contract.

b. Notifications about Corrected Texts
Data: email address.
Purpose: to notify you when a text you reported has been corrected.
Legal Basis: consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

c. Website Analytics
Data: browser and device information, pages visited, referring sites, campaign information, search terms (limited to 80 characters), search results and performance, and time of visit. When you sign in, your account ID links this activity across our website and mobile app. Our self-hosted PostHog service also processes network request information, including IP addresses. For a 5% sample of consenting website sessions, we record interaction replays with page text and form inputs masked to protect private notes, AI questions, and personal information. Network request bodies and console logs are not recorded.
Purpose: to understand and improve website usage, search quality, and use of our services across web and mobile.
Legal Basis: consent. Website analytics starts only after you choose "Allow analytics". You can withdraw using "Cookie preferences" in the footer.

d. Contact via Email
Data: your email address and any other information you provide when you contact info@thaqalayn.com.
Purpose: to respond to your questions or concerns.
Legal Basis: legitimate interest in providing adequate support and communication.

e. Volunteer Applications
Data: account-linked identity and contact data such as your name, username, email address, and user ID, together with the information you submit in the application form, such as country, time zone, Arabic and English proficiency, religious background or school-of-thought details, Arabic and English background, experience, availability, motivation, sample responses, page URL, and technical request metadata such as user agent.
Purpose: to review, process, respond to, and administer volunteer applications and related follow-up communications for roles within Thaqalayn.
Legal Basis: taking steps at your request prior to entering into a volunteer relationship, and our legitimate interest in recruiting, vetting, and coordinating volunteers for our nonprofit activities. Where you provide information revealing religious beliefs or school-of-thought affiliation, we process that information on the basis of your explicit consent as part of your volunteer application submission.

f. ChatGPT Plugin
We receive the search text or source identifier sent by ChatGPT, together with technical request information, to find and return public library sources. Queries may contain personal information if you include it. No Thaqalayn account is required, and the plugin does not access private bookmarks or notes.
Topic searches may send query text to OpenAI to create a search embedding. Our hosting and search services process requests, and Sentry processes operational diagnostics such as errors and performance metrics. Retrieved public sources are returned to ChatGPT. ChatGPT handles your conversation under its own privacy policy and account settings.
We use this information to provide the requested lookup and to maintain the service. The retention, sharing, and deletion rights described in this policy also apply to the plugin.

3. How Long We Keep Your Data
We do not retain your personal data for longer than is strictly necessary to achieve the purposes for which your data is collected, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law.
Account Data: for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will delete your data within a reasonable timeframe unless legal retention obligations apply.
Email Addresses for Notifications: until the notification has been sent, or until you request deletion or withdraw your consent.
Website Analytics Data: retained for a maximum of 26 months. Withdrawing consent stops future collection; you can also contact us to request deletion of previously collected account-linked analytics.
Email Correspondence: for as long as necessary to handle your request and for a reasonable period thereafter for follow-up or archival purposes.
Volunteer Applications: for as long as necessary to review your application, communicate with you about that application, maintain recruitment records, and document volunteer onboarding or rejection decisions, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law.
Plugin Requests and Operational Logs: retained for up to 90 days, then deleted, unless longer retention is required by law.

4. Sharing Personal Data with Third Parties
We do not sell your data to third parties and will only provide it when this is necessary for the execution of our agreement with you, to comply with a legal obligation, or based on our legitimate interests.
We enter into data processing agreements with companies that process your data on our behalf, such as our hosting providers and analytics providers, to ensure the same level of security and confidentiality for your data. We remain responsible for these processing operations.

5. International Data Transfers
Where personal data is transferred internationally, we use appropriate safeguards as required by applicable law.

6. Security of Your Data
We take the protection of your data seriously and apply appropriate technical and organizational measures to prevent misuse, loss, unauthorized access, unwanted disclosure, and unauthorized modification. These measures include SSL/TLS connections, password hashing, and access controls to our systems. If you believe your data is not properly secured, contact info@thaqalayn.com.

7. Cookies
We use cookies on our website. Cookies are small text files that can be stored on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website.
Functional Cookies
These cookies are essential for the website to function properly. For example, we use functional cookies to remember your login session if you have an account. These cookies are placed without requiring your explicit consent.

Analytical Cookies
With your consent, our self-hosted PostHog analytics uses cookies and browser storage to recognize visits and link activity to your account when you sign in. Analytics data is used to improve the site.

Managing Cookies
You can manage or delete cookies through your browser settings. If you block or remove cookies, some parts of the website may not function properly.

8. Your Rights under the GDPR
You have the following rights regarding your personal data:
Right of Access: you can request access to the personal data we process about you.
Right to Rectification: you can request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal data.
Right to Erasure: you can request deletion of your personal data in certain circumstances.
Right to Restriction of Processing: you can request that we limit the way we process your data.
Right to Data Portability: you can request a structured, commonly used, machine-readable copy of the data you provided to us.
Right to Object: you can object to processing based on our legitimate interests.
Right to Withdraw Consent: if processing is based on your consent, you may withdraw it at any time.
To exercise these rights, email info@thaqalayn.com. We will respond as soon as possible and no later than within one month. To verify identity, we may request a redacted copy of an identity document that excludes unnecessary details such as your photo, MRZ, passport number, and national identity number.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be published on our website, and the last updated date at the top of this page will be revised accordingly.

For questions or comments about this Privacy Policy or the processing of your personal data, contact us using the details below.
